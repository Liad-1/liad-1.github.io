Minesweeper (Python)

A JavaScript port of the original Python + pygame game, using its sprites, font and rules.

Left-click to reveal a cell, right-click to flag it. The smiley starts a new game, the gear opens the settings (click it again to save; the game restarts), and Esc pauses. AUTO SOLVER plays by itself: it makes every move the numbers make certain, and when it's stuck it writes each hidden cell's chance of being a mine on the board and guesses the lowest. Click the face to take over with a new game.
