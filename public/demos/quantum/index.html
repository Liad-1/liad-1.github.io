Quantum Demo (Python)

A JavaScript port of the original Python + NumPy + matplotlib animation, with the same gate maths.

A qubit starts in |0⟩ and goes through the gates H, Z, H, drawn as its state vector turning on the Bloch sphere. Play runs the steps, Measure collapses the qubit to |0⟩ or |1⟩ with the probabilities of its current state, and the Speed slider sets the pace. Drag the sphere to rotate the view.
